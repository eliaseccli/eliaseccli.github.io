# SpaceX

Public page `/spacex/` ("SpaceX Launches" in the menu; `/projects/spacex/` and `/projects/starship/` redirect here): a monochrome 3D globe in the style of the SpaceX webcast, showing the next SpaceX launch of any vehicle (Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Starship) with a scrubbable mission clock.

- Mission: Launch Library 2 production (`ll.thespacedevs.com`; not the stale `lldev` sandbox), SpaceX (`lsp__id=121`) upcoming + previous launches, cached in localStorage for 30 min, baked fallback for offline.
- Which launch: every open selects the launch in flight, else the next upcoming one (any vehicle, including classified missions with no orbit data). "Last flight" is the most recent flown launch — also when CelesTrak has not catalogued it yet — and shows only when tapped, or when there is no next launch. Multiple in-flight launches get chips. Nominal profile until CelesTrak has a set. Re-checked every 10 min, so it switches by itself.
- Vehicle: from LL2's rocket configuration (`id` 164 Falcon 9 Block 5, 161 Falcon Heavy, then `families` / `full_name`, so Starship V3 and later configurations need no change; the launch name only for records without a configuration), drawn as a to-scale side view in the attitude widget: Falcon 9 with fairing or with Dragon, Falcon Heavy, Starship (ship + Super Heavy). The SVGs are `<defs>` in `index.html`, one style (`.a-ship`, `.a-dark`, `.a-fin`, `.a-tile`).
- Ascent (altitude and ground speed up to orbit insertion): templates from real flights' telemetry in `ascent-templates.js`, generated and validated by `tools/spacex_ascent.py` (sources and validation errors in its header). `ascent.js` picks one per mission (Starship orbital / suborbital, Falcon Heavy, Falcon 9 ISS / GTO / SSO / LEO), retimes it to the launch's own LL2 timeline (`mode=detailed`, one request for the launch on screen, cached 6 h; template medians otherwise) and rescales the horizontal speed to the mission's azimuth (same inertial speed at SECO). After the data ends it blends over 10 min into a circular orbit model (Falcon: parking orbit, GTO transfer burn between SES-2 and SECO-2; Starship: orbit, deorbit, entry). Speed is relative to the ground, like the webcasts.
- Orbit data, best first: (1) the launch's tracked objects: CelesTrak SATCAT polled by LL2's COSPAR `launch_designator` (empty 200 until catalogued; every 20 min in the first 3 days, hits kept 2 h), then GP by `INTDES` as OMM (6-digit catalogue numbers, converted to TLE with Alpha-5 in `tle.js`); the upper stage (R/B, or Starship's ship) while in orbit, else the median payload. Without a designator yet the last-30-days set by launch date + range. Sets within 3 days of T-0 run SGP4 back to SECO (blended in over 10 min); older ones give only the plane (node regressed with J2). (2) CelesTrak SupGP pre-launch set (FILE= guesses, e.g. `starlink-g15-18`, `starship-flight-16`) close to liftoff. (3) the nominal plane from the pad and LL2 orbit class (Starbase 30.5°, Vandenberg Starlink 53/70/97.6°, Florida Starlink 43/53°, ISS 51.6°, SSO 97.6°, GTO via a ~28.5° parking orbit). The source line says which: `Nominal profile · <template> · <incl>°`, `Pre-launch TLE (…, epoch …)`, `Tracked (TLE <object>, <COSPAR>, upper stage|payload, epoch …)`. Re-checked every 10 min; a new set or newer epoch glides the path over 2.5 s.
- Earth: NASA Blue Marble (public domain), pre-desaturated into `assets/earth-mono-*.jpg`.
- Vendor: three.js r170 (MIT) and satellite.js 5.0.0 (MIT) from the shared `/vendor/`; Barlow font (OFL).
- URL params: `?mission=next|latest`, `?t=<seconds from T-0>`, `?fixture=f14-prelaunch|f14-tracked|florida|florida-hls|f9-vandenberg|f9-florida|f9-iss|f9-gto|fh|two-flying|last-flight`. Debug: `window.__spacex()`.
- Code: `mission.js` is the mission model without the page (launch parsing, orbit plane, trajectory); `spacex.js` the data loaders, globe and HUD. A vehicle that stays in orbit (every Falcon) keeps moving past the drawn path: SGP4 with a set, else the orbit model in closed form; live, the clock is not held at the slider's end. Only Starship stops, at its landing point.
- Tests: `node --test test_pick_launch.js test_ascent.js test_tle.js test_mission.js` (run by `tools/sitecheck.py`). Templates: `python3 tools/spacex_ascent.py` (downloads its sources once into `~/.cache/eliaseccli-spacex`), `--check` to validate without writing.
