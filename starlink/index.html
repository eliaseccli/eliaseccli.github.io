Scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag to pan. Double-click to reset.

Color coding: Hue denotes the shell, transparency the distance from the median altitude in that shell.

In history mode, color is inclination: 43° emerald, 53° orange, 70° cyan, 97° violet.

Daily now-view orbital data from Celestrak, refreshed once a day. Historical frames are derived from Space-Track TLEs. Axes are Anomaly and RAAN.

This site is a progression of the original animations I created showing the Starlink Constellation build-out.
